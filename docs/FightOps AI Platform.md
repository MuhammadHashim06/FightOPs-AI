# FightOps AI Platform  
## Product Scope & Feature Roadmap

---

# 1. Current Scope (MVP)

## 1.1 Matchmaking Operations

### Overview
FightOps AI helps organizations efficiently create fights by managing fighter selection, offers, replacements, and fight confirmation.

### Key Features

- Build fights using:
  - Organization's own contracted fighters
  - External fighter database containing 1,000+ fighters from different European countries

- AI-powered fighter matching based on:
  - Weight class
  - Fighter availability
  - Fight record
  - Location
  - Organization requirements

- Send fight offers directly to:
  - Fighters
  - Fighter managers

- Track offer lifecycle:
  - Sent
  - Pending
  - Accepted
  - Declined

- Automatically find replacement fighters when:
  - Fighter rejects an offer
  - Fighter withdraws from the fight

- Once confirmed:
  - Automatically add fighters to the fight card
  - Trigger all related Fighter Operations workflows

# 3. Fighter Operations

## Overview
Once a fight is confirmed, FightOps AI automatically manages the fighter preparation journey until the fighter reaches:

**"Ready to Fight" Status**

---

## 3.1 Fighter Communication & Onboarding

FightOps AI:

- Contacts fighters automatically
- Explains required next steps
- Requests required information
- Guides fighters through completion process

---

## 3.2 Contract Management

Organizations can:

- Upload contract templates
- Define variable clauses

AI automatically prepares contracts by filling:

- Fighter purse
- Number of fights
- Contract duration
- Exclusivity terms
- Other fighter-specific conditions

Additional capabilities:

- Send contracts through e-signature
- Track signatures
- Send reminders
- Store signed contracts securely

---

## 3.3 Visa & International Fighter Support

For international fighters, AI:

- Determines visa requirements
- Checks event country and fighter nationality
- Identifies required documents
- Generates invitation documents
- Flags visa risks before deadlines

---

## 3.4 Document & Requirement Management

System collects:

### Personal Information

- ID / Passport
- Photos
- Clothing sizes
- Walkout song
- Sponsor logos
- Coach information
- Cornerman details

### Medical Documents

- Bloodwork
- Ophthalmology reports
- ECG
- MRI
- Other required medical records

AI manages:

- Deadlines
- Reminders
- Missing documents
- Expired documents
- Compliance checking

---

## 3.5 Weight Management

During fight week, AI:

- Requests fighter weight updates
- Compares weight against agreed limits
- Detects risk of missing weight
- Alerts operations team

If required:

- Calculates possible deductions
- Sends warnings to fighters

---

## 3.6 Social Media Obligation Tracking

FightOps AI:

- Tracks contractual social media requirements
- Creates schedules
- Sends reminders
- Tracks completion
- Flags missing obligations

---

## 3.7 AI Escalation System

AI handles normal situations automatically.

Human involvement is required only for:

- Contract exceptions
- Medical issues
- Special approvals
- Rule exceptions

All documents are securely organized by:

- Event
- Fighter
